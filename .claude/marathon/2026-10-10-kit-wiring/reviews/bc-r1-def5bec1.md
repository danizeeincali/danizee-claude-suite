On ask it is told to put the question to the owner, but a background agent cannot hold a conversation with the owner and nothing tells the lead to relay the ask or resume the push. — fix: On ask, the agent stops and puts the gate's reason in the Phase 4 summary as 'not pushed — gate asks: \<reason\>'; the lead relays that to the owner and runs the push in the foreground only after they answer.
